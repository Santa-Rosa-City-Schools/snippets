Initial ELPAC: TBD Students - Run to identify TBD students upon enrollment

```text
LIST STU LAC STU.ID STU.CID STU.LN STU.FN STU.SC? STU.GR? STU.ED STU.LF LAC.EAC IF LAC.EAC = TBD
```
