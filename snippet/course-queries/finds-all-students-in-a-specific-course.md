Finds all students in a specific course - Will find all students in course NH098. Change number to change course

```text
LIST STU SEC MST FTF SSE STF STU.ID STU.LN STU.FN SEC.SC CRS CRS.CO CRS.CN STF.LN STF.FN IF CRS.CN = NH098
```
