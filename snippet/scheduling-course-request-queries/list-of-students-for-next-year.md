List of students for next year - Run at the district level. Will give you all students for next year. You must replace XX with your school number

```text
LIST STU STU.ID STU.LN STU.FN STU.NG? STU.SC? STU.NS? IF STU.NG # "13" AND ( STU.NS = 12 OR ( STU.TG = "*" AND STU.NS = 12 )  )
```
