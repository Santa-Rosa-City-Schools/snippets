```text
LIST PWA PWS STU STU.ID STU.LN STU.FN STU.SC? STU.GR PWA.EM PWA.TY PWA.LC PWA.LDT PWA.CD IF PWA.TY = "P" AND PWA.LDT > MM/DD/YYYY
```
