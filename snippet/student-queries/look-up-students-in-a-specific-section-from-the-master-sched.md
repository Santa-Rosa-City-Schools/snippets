Look up students in a specific section from the Master Schedule (make sure to run at site level in Aeries) 
Replace XXX with section number

```text
LIST STU SEC MST FTF SSE STF STU.ID STU.LN STU.FN STU.GR MST.SC IF MST.SC = XXX
```
