List of D and F grades for Fall Semester - 21. Change the 21 for other years.

*In Aeries, the year is the start of the school year, not the end.

```text
LIST STU HIS STU.ID STU.LN STU.FN STU.GR? HIS.YR HIS.TE HIS.MK CRS.CO IF HIS.YR = "21" AND ( HIS.TE = "1" OR HIS.TE = "2" ) AND ( HIS.MK = "F" OR HIS.MK : "D" )
```
