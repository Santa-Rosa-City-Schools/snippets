So that sites and departments can easily locate a list of their former LTEL students

```text
LIST STU LAC STU.SC STU.ID STU.NM STU.GR LAC.EAC LAC.RD1 LAC.LT LAC.YEL IF LAC.EAC = "RFEP" AND LAC.LT = "L"
```
