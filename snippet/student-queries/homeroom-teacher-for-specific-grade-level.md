Homeroom Teacher for Specific Grade Level
Replace XX in "NHHXX" with desired Grade Level (ie: NHH09 will give you all freshmen)

```text
LIST STU SEC MST FTF SSE STF STU.ID STU.LN STU.FN STU.GR CRS CRS.CO CRS.CN STF.LN STF.FN BY STF.LN IF MST.CN = NHHXX
```
