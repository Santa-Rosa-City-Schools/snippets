Next Year Students Leaving - Run at your site. Next school is listed if the student is leaving your school.

```text
LIST STU STU.ID STU.LN STU.FN STU.NG? STU.SC? STU.NS? IF STU.SC # STU.NS AND STU.NS # '' AND ( STU.NG # "13"  OR  STU.TG = "*" )
```
